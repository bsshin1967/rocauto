Business Goal
As part of the metropolitan area air environment improvement project, we are carrying out an engine replacement subsidy program to reduce construction machinery emissions.
Target Area
Nationwide (From Seoul to Jeju)
Target Models
Forklifts manufactured before 2006 (up to 2006 models with identical engine types)
Engine Replacement Time
Takes 3 days

100% Government Supported

Q & A

Q. How much does the replacement cost?
A. It is fully supported for free as an environmental ministry project, provided on a first-come, first-served basis within the budget limit.
Q. How is A/S supported after replacement?
A. It is handled at ROC engine installation shops in each region and supported free of charge for 1 year after modification.
Q. Where is the engine replacement done?
A. The replacement is safely done at ROC installation shops in each region.
Q. Where can I buy consumables?
A. They can be purchased at regional installation shops, ROC headquarters, or SsangYong parts dealerships.
