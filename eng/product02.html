CHP 50kW

CHP (Combined Heat & Power) – 50kW

- It generates power by driving the generator using engine power using CNG engine that uses city gas as fuel. To improve overall efficiency, it has an exhaust gas heat exchanger for recovering high temperature exhaust gas generated during engine operation and a heat exchanger for recovering engine cooling heat.
- 50kW engine is for small-sized combined heat and power generation. V8 gasoline engine is developed and used as a CNG engine. It has a modular configuration that integrates the exhaust gas heat exchanger.
