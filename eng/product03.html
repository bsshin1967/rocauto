CHA 300Ps

CHA (Combined Heat & Air) – 300Ps

- CHA that produces compressed air by driving an airend using engine power by using a CNG engine that uses city gas as fuel. It replaces the air compressor with high demand for power in mid-to-large sized plants and has an exhaust gas heat exchanger for recovering high temperature exhaust gas generated during engine operation and a heat exchanger for recovering engine cooling heat to supply high temperature medium temperature water and steam.
- 200kW engine uses a an engine dedicated to CNG and has a modular configuration that integrates the exhaust gas heat exchanger
