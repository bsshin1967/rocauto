Measures to Improve Customer Satisfaction

Improved Maintainability and Reduced Customer Maintenance/Repair Costs
    • If a vehicle intake is necessary due to a quality issue within the warranty period, we provide our own alternative forklift, fundamentally resolving customer inconvenience during the repair period. (Spare vehicles: 5 units)


Convenience of Recognition for Vehicle Owners
    • Installation of a diagnostic module including an engine warning light (driver's seat), warning/notifying the driver when an engine problem occurs to prevent escalation into a major failure.
    • The replacement engine is a SsangYong Motor applied engine (Rexton), so in case of failure, inspection is possible at nearby general auto repair centers, minimizing down time. ( Using general vehicle diagnostics)


Improved Maintainability
    • Easy inspection/repair through diagnosis of engine parts such as injectors, turbochargers, and fuel pressure using an engine diagnostic tool (Failure diagnosis items: 331)
    • When repairing such as replacing parts, supply and demand are smooth with general auto parts, and it is relatively cheap, reducing maintenance/repair costs. (Using general vehicle diagnostics)
